# AICO-006 AEO Foundations Audit

## Scope and verdict

- **Audit role:** AEO Foundations Architecture
- **Reviewed package:** proposed ADR-008, `POLICY_APPROVAL.md`,
  `AICO_006_THREAT_TEST_PLAN.md`, `AICO_006_EVIDENCE.md`, ADR-005,
  `OBSERVABILITY_AND_EVALUATION.md`, and the accepted AICO-002/AICO-003
  boundaries
- **Authority:** `duckvhuynh/aicompanyos#6`, decision child
  `duckvhuynh/aico-backend#12`, bounded proof child
  `duckvhuynh/aico-backend#13`, SRS-FR-021-026/085-088, SRS-FR-087,
  AT-004-005, and SRS TD-007
- **Verdict:** **Conditionally conformant as an architecture package; not
  implementation-, approval-, MVP-, or private-alpha-ready.**

The proposed package is discoverable and reviewable as a linked set of stable
contracts, threat cases, and evidence IDs. It separates PostgreSQL authority from
telemetry, binds founder decisions to exact versions, and keeps denial audit
evidence from becoming permission. Current schema and runtime behavior are partial
reusable evidence only. Decision child #12 may select the architecture after the
required attributable approvals; proof child #13 must then produce the bounded,
offline evidence. Production policy, decision-service, and founder-control behavior
remain owned by AICO-031, AICO-041, and AICO-042 respectively.

## Evidence retention and redaction rules

AICO-006 evidence MUST retain only:

- stable gate/threat ID, exact repository SHA, verification run/attempt ID, and
  immutable fixture, migration, schema, workflow, policy, employee, environment,
  budget, and image manifest references;
- bounded UTC interval, result and safe reason class, command/input/output digests,
  replay mode, synthetic before/after counts, causal ID graph, fake-adapter and
  budget aggregate counts, evidence checksum, and attributable review reference;
- classification, authorized audience, producer/check version, retention class,
  policy version, and expiry/hold state.

It MUST NOT retain credentials, cookies, authorization headers, identity subject,
raw policy input or parameters, artifact/attachment/source bodies, founder feedback,
prompts/completions/transcripts, hidden reasoning, object keys, provider/tool
payloads, unrestricted stack traces/SQL parameters, foreign-tenant identifiers, or
raw receipt content outside its authorized audience. Unknown fields, a seeded
canary match, failed classification/audience check, unbounded output, or uncertain
redaction drops the payload and fails the applicable gate. A denial audit required
by SRS-FR-087 keeps only the authenticated own-scope categorical/digested facts
needed to explain the decision; it never copies a forged foreign target into an
audience-visible event.

Evidence retention follows a versioned finite policy and the accepted AICO-003
mechanism. DEC-013 remains open, so this audit defines no duration and makes no
Product, legal, deletion, or backup promise.

## Four safe replay and reproduction modes

| Mode                         | Permitted behavior                                                                                                                                                                                   | Prohibited behavior                                                                                                                                      | AICO-006 owner/proof                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `STATE_RECONSTRUCTION`       | Read PostgreSQL decisions, exact artifact references, ordered events/outbox, idempotency records, continuation keys, and immutable manifests to report the current legal gate state and causal gaps. | Mutation, new policy evaluation, continuation dispatch, external call, inferred success, or replacement of missing evidence.                             | Child #13 restart/redelivery reconstruction; AICO-041 production diagnostic/read path.                                                          |
| `OFFLINE_REPRODUCTION`       | On a clean exact SHA, evaluate an immutable synthetic command and policy manifest with frozen clock/IDs and fake adapters in disposable local services, producing a separate evidence identity.      | Product mutation, internet/paid/provider/tool/Designer call, use of real founder data, or a claim that an opaque external model is bitwise reproducible. | Child #13 deterministic fixture; AICO-031/AICO-041 contract suites.                                                                             |
| `CONTROLLED_REEVALUATION`    | Create a new evaluation identity against immutable prior inputs using an explicitly selected new evaluator/policy/schema manifest; compare results while preserving both lineages.                   | Rewriting or upgrading the original PolicyDecision, founder decision, receipt, event, artifact, policy manifest, or acceptance evidence.                 | AICO-031 policy rollout/evaluation tooling and later quality/release issues; not required to claim child #13 complete unless implemented there. |
| `SIDE_EFFECT_RECONCILIATION` | With separate authorization, inspect a previously recorded external tool/provider/budget effect by stable invocation/idempotency identity and append the observed outcome and recovery decision.     | Blind retry, new effect under old authority, double charge, changed tenant/parameters/digest, or treating unknown as success.                            | Later tool/runtime/budget operations; child #13 proves only that its fake external-call ledger remains zero for the decision boundary.          |

Command retry is not automatically one of these modes. Same idempotency key plus
the same stable business digest returns the original committed receipt; changed
business content conflicts. Transport/event redelivery materializes at most the
already-authorized logical continuation and cannot create new authority.

## Audit conclusion

No AEO foundations objection blocks presenting the AICO-006 package for the
required human architecture and Product/Security decisions, provided acceptance
preserves all twelve gates above. The package remains architecture-ready only. A
claim that the current schema, hard-coded deterministic-provider `ALLOW`, proposed
receipt, fake ledgers, green CI, or agent review proves implementation, MVP, or
private-alpha readiness would fail `A6-AEO-01`, `A6-AEO-03`, `A6-AEO-06`,
`A6-AEO-08`, `A6-AEO-10`, and `A6-AEO-12`.
