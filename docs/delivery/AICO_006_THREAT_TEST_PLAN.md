# AICO-006 Release-Blocking Threat and Evidence Plan

## Status, purpose, and authority

- **Status:** Proposed for AICO-006 Architecture and Product/Security acceptance
- **Decision child:**
  [aico-backend#12](https://github.com/duckvhuynh/aico-backend/issues/12)
- **Bounded proof child:**
  [aico-backend#13](https://github.com/duckvhuynh/aico-backend/issues/13)
- **Parent:** [aicompanyos#6](https://github.com/duckvhuynh/aicompanyos/issues/6)
- **Release classification:** Release-blocking at R0 for the accepted decision and
  bounded proof; later production surfaces remain blocked by their named release
  gates.
- **Requirement trace:** `G-02`, `G-05`, `MVP-CAP-004`, policy portion of
  `MVP-CAP-011`, `PRD-FR-016` through `PRD-FR-020`, `PRD-FR-059` through
  `PRD-FR-060`, `SRS-FR-021` through `SRS-FR-026`, `SRS-FR-085` through
  `SRS-FR-088`, `AT-004`, and `AT-005`.

This plan is the proposed adversarial and evidence contract for selecting a
deny-by-default policy and exact-version founder GATE-01 decision architecture. It
becomes binding only after attributable Architecture and separate Product/Security
owners approve the exact semantic SHA. Architecture prose, an agent conclusion, a
model response, a transcript, green CI on another SHA, or an unreviewed demo is not
acceptance or authority.

The production failure question is: **when a stale, duplicated, forged, cross-tenant,
or non-founder request reaches any layer, what prevents it from manufacturing an
approval or starting Designer work?** The answer must be deterministic application
and PostgreSQL enforcement with observable, replay-safe evidence. A model must never
be asked to judge whether its own output is approved.

## Non-negotiable authorization and transaction invariants

1. Founder identity and company ownership are resolved from authenticated,
   server-controlled context. Claimed actor fields in a command, employee output,
   model output, transcript, event, or message cannot elevate authority.
2. Every evaluation uses one canonical, typed input containing actor or exact
   Employee Definition version, company, run, task and attempt where applicable,
   current stage and state plus expected row version, gate, action, exact resource
   and Artifact Version ID plus checksum, approval references, budget snapshot
   digest, environment digest, parameter digest, decision expiry, correlation and
   causation IDs, workflow version, and policy version. Missing, malformed, stale,
   unsupported, or unknown required facts deny.
3. `ALLOW` is tenant-, actor-, action-, attempt-, parameter-, resource-, exact
   artifact-version-, workflow-version-, policy-version-, environment-, budget-, and
   expiry-bound. It is not a session grant and cannot authorize a changed payload.
4. The GATE-01 decision transaction locks and re-resolves current founder/company,
   run, wait/gate, exact pending artifact, workflow, and policy authority. It then
   appends an immutable decision and policy result, an ordered event and outbox row,
   and only the permitted state transition and continuation intent in one atomic,
   idempotent commit.
5. `APPROVE` may move the exact GATE-01 candidate from
   `AWAITING_BRIEF_APPROVAL` to the selected design-ready state and create exactly one
   synthetic downstream intent. `REQUEST_REVISION` preserves the reviewed version,
   stores a typed/redacted founder feedback reference, returns to the selected PM
   revision state, and creates exactly one synthetic revision intent.
6. The AICO-006 proof never executes `EMP-DES`, invokes a model or tool, creates a
   Design Specification, or claims the production decision API/UI complete. The
   synthetic continuation is evidence of an authorized transaction boundary, not
   Designer execution.
7. A denial permits no unauthorized run/task/artifact/approval transition, success
   event/outbox, continuation, provider/model/tool/sandbox call, credential grant,
   budget reservation/consumption, or cost. Where `SRS-FR-087` applies to a
   privileged tool request, denial must still append exactly one scoped/redacted
   `PolicyDecision` plus its linked denial event/outbox; those audit rows are not an
   unauthorized business or external effect.
8. Employees exchange schema-valid, immutable, typed artifact references and task
   results only. Free-form prose and historical transcripts are data, never commands,
   approvals, policy decisions, or continuation authority.
9. Terminal or canceled runs never reopen and never create new work. A restart or
   transport replay reconstructs and continues only from committed PostgreSQL state;
   process memory and queue presence have no authority.

## Deterministic two-company fixture

Every executable case uses one paid-service-free fixture created from a clean
checkout:

- synthetic Company A and Company B, each with a distinct authenticated founder,
  immutable marker values, and disjoint runs, tasks, attempts, artifacts, decisions,
  idempotency keys, correlations, and event sequences;
- Company A has one GATE-01 run in `AWAITING_BRIEF_APPROVAL`, a current immutable
  Product Brief version, at least one stale version, an open typed wait/checkpoint,
  and pinned workflow, policy, employee, schema, budget, and environment versions;
- Company B has equivalent but distinguishable records for cross-tenant and
  non-disclosure tests; random, malformed, deleted, expired, and unknown identifiers
  are also generated deterministically;
- a frozen UTC clock, deterministic UUID/key source, pinned migrations and local
  container images, and fake provider/tool/Designer/continuation ledgers whose call
  count begins at zero;
- separate application, worker, outbox-consumer, and least-privilege database
  identities. Direct SQL attacks use the ordinary application/service identities,
  never a migration owner or superuser as proof of runtime protection; and
- no real founder data, credential, paid service, external model/provider/tool,
  ambient cloud resource, shared developer database, or internet dependency.

The fixture exchanges typed Artifact Version references and canonical digests. It
does not inject a model transcript into an instruction channel. An attempted
external or paid call, any actual Designer execution, an unclassified side effect,
or a fixture canary in retained output fails the run.

## Common execution and evidence protocol

For every row, the harness captures the authoritative before-state, executes the
action through the named boundary, and captures the committed after-state. Evidence
must include the exact clean repository SHA, command, case ID, fixture and schema
versions, migration version, policy/workflow/employee versions, process identities,
bounded UTC interval, input and output digests, safe result class, database invariant
counts, ordered state/event sequence, outbox/inbox result, fake-adapter call ledgers,
budget diff, and redaction scan.

The expected rejection is a stable, non-disclosing denial or conflict. Foreign and
unknown resources must have equivalent status, schema, safe reason class, and no
tenant/resource existence detail. HTTP or service response assertions alone are not
sufficient; PostgreSQL, event/outbox, continuation, invocation, and budget evidence
must agree.

Each case produces one of `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `BLOCKED`. Missing, skipped, flaky,
timed-out, quarantined, unowned, unredacted, or unavailable evidence is `FAIL` at the
applicable gate. A retry is a new evidence attempt and cannot overwrite the first
failure. No manual spot check, schedule waiver, or model-authored assertion can
convert missing evidence into a pass.

## Evidence handling and non-waivable exit

Retained evidence contains only synthetic references, stable case/reason IDs,
versions, canonical digests, bounded timestamps/durations, aggregate counts, and
checksums. Raw artifact or feedback bodies, prompts/completions, transcripts, hidden
reasoning, credentials, cookies/tokens, stack traces, raw storage keys, signed URLs,
provider bodies, and other-company metadata are prohibited. A positive canary scan
fails closed; any necessary unredacted investigation belongs in a separately
authorized, access-restricted evidence store.

AICO-006 cannot pass its R0 gate unless all of the following are true:

1. Architecture and Product/Security owners accept the exact decision and this plan
   on the exact semantic SHA, with disputes and conditions resolved.
2. The document validator, deliberate-failure probes, canonical `npm run verify:ci`,
   and exact-final-SHA hosted CI pass from a clean checkout.
3. aico-backend#13 runs every case assigned to its bounded proof with no skip,
   unknown effect, external/paid call, Designer execution, redaction failure, or
   unresolved Critical/High finding.
4. QA/Security independently verifies the exact-final-SHA evidence, the two-company
   non-disclosure result, the decision/event/outbox causation chain, restart/replay
   invariants, fake-adapter zeros, and budget diff.
5. Every production remainder stays open and release-blocking under AICO-031,
   AICO-041, or AICO-042; no architecture, spike, green test, or human approval is
   relabeled as implementation evidence outside its stated boundary.

Any unauthorized founder decision, cross-tenant disclosure, artifact-history rewrite,
Designer/model/tool invocation, duplicate continuation, unbounded authority, missing
denial evidence required by `SRS-FR-087`, or unknown external/cost effect is Critical
and non-waivable. Any semantic change to policy inputs, decision transaction,
workflow/gate rules, artifact types, fixture, validator, or evidence schema requires a
new exact SHA, complete deterministic rerun, and fresh applicable human acceptance.
